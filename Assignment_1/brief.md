# Project Brief — Kissan Mitra

## What is the project?

Kissan Mitra is a farmer-to-buyer marketplace concept intended to help farmers sell agricultural products directly to buyers.

## Who are the users?

- Farmers who want to list and sell their agricultural products.
- Buyers who want to discover and purchase agricultural products.
- Administrators who may manage users, listings, and platform activity.

## Main Nouns

- Farmer
- Buyer
- Product
- Order
- Payment
- User
- Marketplace
- Administrator

## Main Verbs

- Register
- Login
- List products
- Search products
- View product details
- Place orders
- Manage listings
- Track orders

## Purpose

The goal is to create a simple, accessible marketplace that connects farmers with buyers and supports direct agricultural trade.
